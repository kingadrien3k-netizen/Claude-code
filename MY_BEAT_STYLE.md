# My Rap Beat Style

My lane: emotional, melodic street rap ("pain rap"). Every beat is new, never recycled, but they all share this feel.

## Always
- Instrumental, about 1:30 to 1:50 (ask vidIQ for 105 seconds)
- 145-152 BPM, half-time bounce
- Deep, punchy 808s
- Crisp trap hi-hat rolls and a hard clap or snare
- Airy pads and soft vocal-chop textures
- Sad, heartfelt, late-night mood
- Short intro, full drop, outro

## Favorite so far: Pain Rap 04 - Bells (146 BPM)
Nostalgic electric piano chords, warm bell lead melody, dreamy reverb pads, airy vocal chops, deep rumbling 808s, hopeful but hurting mood. Lean this way.

## Also a hit: Pain Rap 08 - ATL Underground (160 BPM, F minor)
Babystaydown vibe. Atlanta underground melodic trap: ethereal sped-up soulful melody loop, dreamy plucks, pitched vocal chops, hard knocking distorted 808s, bouncy skippy hi-hats. Drums in by about 3 seconds, one small switch-up, then it rides.

## Base prompt
Emotional melodic trap beat, instrumental, soulful pain-rap vibe, [LEAD MELODY], airy atmospheric pads and soft vocal-chop textures, deep punchy 808 bass, crisp trap hi-hat rolls and hard clap snare, half-time bounce, [MOOD] late-night mood, [145-152] BPM, polished professional mix with a short intro, full drop, and outro

## How I want beats made
- One beat at a time. Make one, let me listen, then make the next.
- Never recycle a beat. Every one is brand new in this style.
- Don't mention credits or how many beats are left.
- No long bridges or breakdowns. Keep the beat driving the whole way (Pain Rap 05 missed because of this).
- Always open with a spacey, galaxy-sounding intro like Pain Rap 04. That intro is the feeling.
- Samples and sounds are fine. Just never recycle a whole beat.
- Goal: I own the songs I rap on.
- Only make a beat when I ask for one. Never start one on your own.
- One switch-up max per beat. Pain Rap 06 switched up 3 times and that was too much.
- Keep the intro short, about 4 seconds before the drums drop.
- Beat switches need to hit harder. Pain Rap 08's switch at 0:29 was too weak, just a quick drop-out. Make the switch a real change (new melody layer, 808 pattern change, or a hard drum-out into a bigger drop).
- Beat switches are NOT required every time. Default is no switch, one steady groove. Only add a switch when I ask, and if I do, make it hit hard.
- Default lane is STREET Atlanta (Babystaydown, Lil Tony, Fatt Mack street side). Keep it gritty, not soft or "girl beat" sounding, unless I ask for a love/R&B one.
- The BOUNCE matters most: Pain Rap 04's groove (146 BPM half-time, rumbling 808s locked with the kick, rolling hats) made me bob my head naturally. Every street ATL beat needs that head-nod bounce, not stiff or corny.
- Street ATL 01 (158 BPM) missed: too fast and frantic, no head-nod bounce. Keep street beats closer to 146 BPM half-time with the Pain Rap 04 pocket.
- Doesn't have to be street ATL. "Heavenly" is the vibe: angelic, airy, dreamy, glowing (like Pain Rap 04's galaxy feel), keeping the 04 head-nod bounce.
